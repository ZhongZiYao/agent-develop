The Mall (商店街) is accessible from the Courtyard Scroll menu or Town. Here players can buy a variety of items such as illustrations, souls, skins, S-Jade, and packs among other things. To access the shops, players can tap on the icons on the map or select their icons on the bottom of the screen.

Features without direct buttons that can only be clicked on from the mall map are Ebisu's Boutique & Lucky Prize Draw.

- *This feature is only available on CN servers.*

Will lead to the real-life online shop, where players can buy merchandise with CNY currency. Players can also access the website via http://gmall.yys.163.com/.

The Consignment Shop allows players to trade S-Jade & special tickets for certain Skins, Skin Shards, Shards, & Accessories.

These tickets are Consignment Tickets & Jade Tickets. Consignment Tickets are used to purchase shards and accessories and maybe be purchased with S-Jade & Jade. Jade Tickets are received from doing Consignment Shop sales and can be used to exchange for Gold/Coins & Jade.

At the time only limited items are available for consignment, such as; collaboration shikigami shards, collaboration shikigami skin shards, and skin vouchers.

Only certain newer skins may offer these skin vouchers that can then be sold in the consignment shop 30 days after purchase.

To consign a shikigami shard, the player must already possess the shikigami and have not received the shards from "wishing" or "donation". These shards can then be sold and bought for Consignment Tickets.

- *For more information about loading screen illustrations, see Collection.*

Illustrations can be purchased with Jade or Illustration Vouchers and then set them a the loading screen.

Illustrations are available for a limited time before the shop is refreshed with a new set. Illustrations may be cycled back in at random or included in special Summon Events. The refresh date for the shop can be found in the bottom right of the page, and it usually coincides with the release dates for new SSR/SP shikigami.

Occasionally, additional illustrations not part of a current set are added to coincide with the release of newly released S-Jade skins or the end of a large story events. These additional illustrations have separate refresh dates to whatever the current illustration set is.

The Mystic Trader can be found in Town and also has shortcut through Mall.

Items can be exchanged for Souls.

A random Grade-6 Soul can be purchased in exchange for 50 Orochi Scales, has a weekly limit of 40.

A Soul of choice of type and slot can be purchased in exchange for 50 Reverse Orochi Scales.

A Boss Soul of choice of type and slot can be purchased in exchange for 50 Demon Souls, has a weekly limit of 50.

A random Grade-6 Soul the player can pick of 3 randomly selected options which Soul they want in exchange for 200 Forgotten Books, has a weekly limit of 30.

A special "dark" version skin for all 4 Onmyouji Totems. Challenge the Totem Zone from Tuesday to Sunday for materials to unlock them.

When enough items are gathered, a selected emoji can be unlocked. Each emoji costs 20 Totem Talisman, 60 Soul Talisman, and 30 Evo Talisman to unlock that can be found in their respective instances. Voiced emoji can be sampled first.

The shop will show random souls and will refresh every 60 minutes with new offers (starting from the first time it is visited upon login). Offers can be refreshed at any time by using 3 jade at the top-right corner. There is a limit of 500 refreshes a day. Around the refresh button is a fortune counter. When players refresh 10 times to fill the counter, refresh one final time for guaranteed grade 6 souls (noting restrictions below).

The shop may work differently in times of events.


Sells skins for onmyouji and shikigami. Skins can be previewed by looking at the still art and 3D model in 360 degrees, as well as purchased with Skin Tickets, Jade or S-Jade depending on the tier. Purchases are one-time.

The different tiers of skins are Normal skins, Premium skins, Special skins, and Deluxe skins. Normal ones have no additional effects only change the look of a shikigami. Premium skins only change a single skill's VFX effects and that upgrade costs S-Jade while the skin itself will only cost Skin Tickets. Special skins can only be bought with S-Jade and change VFX effects of skills and come with a frame. Deluxe skins can only be bought with S-Jade, come with even more add-ons, and are only available for limited periods of time. For more about them check the Deluxe Skins page.

Players also have the option to purchase skins with S-Jade as gifts for friends, limited to needing to be on the same server.

To use a shikigami skin players have to obtain the shikigami first. Players can buy skins for shikigami they still don't have, it will be stored until they obtain them.
