Skills are the various abilities of characters in the game.

They are called "demonic arts" (妖術) when referring to special skills of shikigami, and the skills of onmyouji are called "onmyoudo" (陰陽道).

Under auto mode, two types of skills can be toggled: normal attacks and special skills. Normal attacks are skill 1 and consume no onibi. Special skills tend to be skill 3, but can be skill 2 as well, and have effects other than simply dealing damage.

Certain skills have a chance to automatically activate without consuming any onibi nor needing any controls once their requirements are met. These skills are usually called passives, which tend to be skill 2, however it is possible for normal and special skills to have similar properties.

Skills have unique names, icons, and voiced lines from their correlating characters.

Skill effects can be either shikigami-specific or use a common mechanism. Specific skill mechanisms are denoted in red text, while common effects appear in blue text.

Buff effects are usually denoted by blue icons and debuff effects by red icons, while indirect damage effects have purple icons (excluding Ghostly Songstress's seduced debuff). There are also special effects that are denoted by yellow icons. Special effects are usually considered to be Exclusive effects that are unremovable or "permanent" until the certain conditions are no longer met, eg; start a new round in a multi-round battle.

Numbers at the top-left of an icon represent how many stacks of the same effect there are. Numbers at the top-right of an icon represent how many layers/levels/stacks an effect has. Numbers at the bottom-right represent how many turns the effect will last or be sustained for. Effects that only last or are sustained for 1 turn may not have a number at the bottom. In unique circumstances, such as Yuki Onna's secret zone, numbers at the bottom represent how many turns until the effect will take place.

Skills may or may not have several levels, where levelling up the skill makes it stronger.

For onmyouji, skills can be levelled by unlocking achievements and from levelling their totem.

For shikigami, skills can be levelled by consuming black daruma or another copy of the shikigami while levelling or upgrading them.

For shikigami with changeable skills or additional skills, such as Takiyasha Hime and Kidoumaru, those skills are levelled by upgrading the main skill. Some may even share the same upgrade effects. So a skill with 2 changeable special attacks and 4 levels on each will only need a total of 4 upgrades, not 8.
