My Heart Resonates (我心共呜)

Exclusive only. At the beginning of the battle, gain 1 Onibi. Gain 5 stacks of Resonate at the start of the battle and at the beginning of each turn.

- Resonate (共呜)
- File:Buff218.png
- **Buff, Mark** : Max 5 stacks. Any non-summoned ally that takes damage that exceeds past 10% of their max HP will lose 1 stack and will recover HP equal to 8% of Luo Tianyi's max HP. (Every ally can only receive Resonate once per turn.)
- Recover (恢復)
- Different from healing or restoring, cannot Crit, unaffected by reduced healing effects and does not trigger effects related to healing.

| Level | Effect | 
|---|---|
| **2** | Resonate's HP recovery increases to 10%. | 
| **3** | Resonate's HP recovery increases to 12%. | 
| **4** | Resonate's HP recovery increases to 15%. | 
| **5** | All allies Crit DMG is increased by 30%. | 


Tianyi's Manifestation (天依万象)

Exclusive only. Luo Tianyi receives Rhythm Change and increases all ally's Crit DMG to 15%, maintained for 1 turn.

- Rhythm Change (韵转)
- File:Unique92.png
- **Buff, Mark** : At the end of the turn, the non-summoned ally at the top of the move bar is moved, increasing their move bar by 25%, and restores their HP up to 8% of Luo Tianyi's max HP, this effect lasts until Luo Tianyi's turn. (This effect can be triggered a maximum amount of 8 times between turns.)

| Level | Effect | 
|---|---|

| **4** | When cast, all ally's Crit DMG is increased by 30%, maintained for 1 turn. |
