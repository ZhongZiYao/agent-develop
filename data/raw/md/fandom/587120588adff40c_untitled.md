Items can be obtained from certain zones, events, and as rewards for completing Achievements or Daily Missions. They can also be purchased in the Mall.

| Name | Description | 
|---|---|
| AP | AP is required to access various PVE instances such as Orochi Soul Zones, Evo Zones, Explore Zones and Shikigami Dispatch. If players have under 100 AP, it will naturally recover by 1 per 5 minutes. | 
| Sakuramochi | An item required to feed the Paper Doll for the auto function. Feeding it grants the Paper Doll 100 AP and it can be fed a max of 5 times per day. The 1st and 2nd times require 10 Sakuramochi, the 3rd and 4th requires 20, and the 5th requires 30. | 
| EXP Pamphlets | An item that can be fed to the Gorging Ghoul. Each pamphlet can fill it up with a set amount of EXP. The quantity differs depending on the pamphlet grade. |
