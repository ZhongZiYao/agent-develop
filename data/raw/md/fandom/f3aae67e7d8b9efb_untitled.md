Fate's Favor (时也运也)

Exclusive effect. At the start of battle and at each turn, Fusoukozen gains or refreshes Phantom Dice.

- Upon Usage
- He has a 50% base chance to inflict Fortune Dice on enemy shikigami until the shield from Phantom Dice disappears.
- Phantom Dice (幻象骰子)
- **Buff, Mark** : Shield that cannot be dispelled. Absorbs damage equal to 450% of starting ATK.
- Fortune Dice (运势骰子)
- **General, Mark** : Swaps all stats except for SPD, the swapped stats cannot exceed 330% of this unit's own starting stats. Fusoukozen is unaffected by this effect. Before a target with this effect takes action, all allies recover HP equal to 98% of Fusoukozen's starting ATK. After the target acts, all enemies lose HP equal to 78% of Fusoukozen's starting ATK (this effect can be triggered three times before Fusoukozen's next action.)

| Level | Effect | 
|---|---|
| **2** | Increases the base chance to 100%. | 
| **3** | When Phantom Dice disappears or refreshes, increases the move bar by 20%. | 
| **4** | Guaranteed to inflict on cast. | 
| **5** | When Phantom Dice disappears or refreshes again, all allies recover HP equal to 98% of Fusoukozen's starting ATK. | 


Fox Trick (狐影戏法)

Fusoukozen attacks all enemies via a magic circle and commands the fox shadow to attack one designated enemy once (if the target is defeated by the magic circle, the attack will shift to the enemy with the lowest HP). If 600% of ATK is higher than HP when this skill is cast, it deals damage equal to 100% and 265% of ATK respectively. Otherwise, it deals damage equal to 10% of 21% of max HP respectively. (The damage dealt does not exceed 1600% of Fusoukozen's initial ATK.)

| Level | Effect | 
|---|---|
| **2** | Fox shadow damage increases to 275% and 24% respectively. | 
| **3** | Dealing damage does not trigger the enemy's soul effects or passives. | 
| **4** | Fox shadow damage increases to 300% and 30% respectively. | 
| **5** | If Fortune Dice is present, reduces onibi cost by 1. |
