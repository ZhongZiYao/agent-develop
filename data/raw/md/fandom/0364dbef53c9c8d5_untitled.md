An organised compilation of Onmyoji's lore and story content. Stories are also accessible from Retrospect via the courtyard tree and are divided into three sections: History, Side Story and The Rival. Currently, the only open section in Retrospect is History which contains the major arcs for Kuro Seimei, Sealand Invasion, Demoniac Feast, and the Takamagahara/Six Realms arc.

Heian Tales and Memory Scrolls mostly provide background information for their related characters. They are not essential to fully understand event stories, but are highly recommended.
