Not to be confused with the shikigami
Orochi
.
You can receive souls from defeating enemies at a location marked Soul (御魂) with three flags on the exploration map, south-west of the capital. Inside you can fight three-round fights, with the first two rounds having enemies determined by the stage, and the last round having Orochi and two more enemies. To unlock access to higher, more difficult stages that yield higher quality souls, previous stages must be cleared.
Stage 11 Orochi of the Maidens: Moans (巫女オロチ・悲鳴), requires players to have cleared stage 10 and cleared the 3 tutorials quests for stage 11.
Stage 12 Archnaga: Judgement (大蛇神·神罚), requires players to be level 40, to have cleared stage 11 and cleared the 3 tutorials quests for stage 12.
Stage 13 Aether Orochi (终焉大蛇·虚无), requires players to be level 45, to have cleared stage 12 and cleared the 3 tutorials quests for stage 13.
Clearing stages 10-13 can cause
Ultimate Orochi
(真オロチ) to appear.

Sea of Eternity (永生之海/永生の海) is unlocked at level 36 and after beating the 10th stage of
Orochi
.
Introduction
[
]
Sea of Eternity is a Soul dungeon that drops souls typically unavailable through challenging
Orochi
, and is an alternative to buying them from the
mall
. The 4 floors of Sea of Eternity all require AP to access. Stage 1 and 2 require 18 and 24 AP respectively, while Stage 3 and 4 require 30 AP. This zone also has its own unique soul drop buff that can only be used 30 times every week.
Format
[
]
Sea of Eternity can be challenged solo or co-op, and consists of 2 rounds. A line up of 5 shikigami and 1 onmyouji are allowed each round, however, the shikigami used in round 1 cannot be used again in round 2. Co-op is limited to 2 people; 1 host and 1 guest. Unlike regular zones, co-op in Sea of Eternity requires each person to take charge of one round instead of combining shikigami to form the team. Therefore, each player must bring an entire team of 5 shikigami and 1 onmyouji for the round they are in charge of. No spectator shikigami are allowed.
Special Mechanics
[
]
The 2 rounds require separate teams that do not use the same shikigami.
Each round has a time limit of 2 minutes. Leftover time from round 1 is added to round 2.
If players fail round 1, they'll start round 2 with little to no time left.
If players fail both rounds, half of the AP used to challenge the zone will be refunded.
This zone has its own unique soul boost that increases the drops for the first 30 successful clears each week. Failure to clear the zone will not count towards  the 30.
