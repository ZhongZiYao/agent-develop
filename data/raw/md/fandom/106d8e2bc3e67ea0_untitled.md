- In the default town (old version) there is a chance of a Hyakki Yakou appearing in the early hours of the morning (3-5 AM).
- In the default town there are various easter eggs.
  - There is a chance that Tamamo no Mae will stand on top of the soul shop building or Ebisu's Boutique.
  - There is a chance that SP Ibaraki Douji and SP Shuten Douji will sit together in front of the building with a oni coming out of it. They will endless repeat their cheer animations, however, SP Ibaraki will sometimes bug out and stand still.
  - There is a chance that Ootengu and Yuki Onna will fly across the sky.
  - There is a chance that Yama Usagi and Mouba will walk up and down the street.
  - There is a chance that Ame Onna will walk up and down the street.
  - There is a chance that Tesso will stand near the building with a oni coming out of it.
  - There is a chance that YYS and Akanama will stand together in front of the building for the general shop. An animation of the soot sprites falling on top of Akaname will endlessly repeat.
  - There is a chance that the PVP judge will stand under the bridge.
  - There is a chance that a large oni will walk up and the down the street.
- In the default town (on extreme quality) there is a giant Ibaball above the sakura tree near the illustration shop.
- In the default town (on extreme quality) there is a small youkai that looks like Kirby in front of Ebisu's Boutique.
- Pinball versions of Yama Usagi and Mouba can be found racing around in the Mall.

- The bells in the default courtyard will change the background music.
- Shaking the phone causes the courtyard tree to drop sakura petals.
- Kappa hides in the pond near the pet house in the summer courtyard skin.
- The Amanojaku/Imp N shikigami can be found hiding in the summer courtyard skin as well.
  - Green Imp can be found hiding behind the sakura tree's trunk.
  - Red Imp can be found hiding in the branches of the sakura tree.
  - Yellow Imp can be found hiding behind Seimei's table.
  - Blue Imp can be found hiding a tree near the lantern rack.
- When the player's character is a shikigami, Kappa and the Imps will not appear in the summer courtyard.
- Hakurou has a small interaction with Hiromasa if she is placed in the default courtyard.
- Kyonshii Imouto will play with the courtyard pet if she is placed in the courtyard.
- When Taishakuten and Asura are left idle in the courtyard together while equipped with their s-jade skins, they'll have a small interaction.
  - Taishakuten will make 1 of 2 comments and Asura will respond with a comment of his own.
- When players have the Melodic Pavilion courtyard equipped and view Shiranui via the display feature in Shikigami Records, players will have an extra option to watch Shiranui's dance.
- In the Celestial Haven courtyard there are various easter eggs:
  - Tapping the lotus flower behind Kagura will trigger a cut scene of Taishakuten dancing. The cut scene can only be triggered during the evening while the flower is glowing.
  - There is a chance Taishakuten will perform a dance as an idle animation when set as the player character.
  - There is a chance Taishakuten will appear on bridge behind the Town sign.
    - When Asura is set as the player character, he is guaranteed to appear on the bridge. When he appears the BGM will change to the track 我心之人_天域篇組曲 and a voice line for Taishakuten will trigger.
    - When Asura is set as the courtyard shikigami and Taishakuten appears on the bridge, the BGM will change to the track 我心之人_天域篇組曲.
  - There is a chance a white swan and a black swan will appear on the right side of the courtyard when Taishakuten is set as the player character.
  - The paper doll who holds shortcuts to events in the top-right of the screen will dress-up as Taishakuten.
  - At night time, small blue spirits will appear around the courtyard.
  - There is a chance the lotus flower behind Kagura will become pink.
- If buffs aren't redeemed from the paper doll in the courtyard, it falls asleep.
- In the CN version of the game it will occasionally rain in the courtyard.
- During the release of certain shikigami or events, small changes will be made the courtyard and town. Eg;
  - During SP Aoandon's release, red butterflies around the courtyard tree and lanterns changed to blue butterflies.
  - During Kinnara's release, sound effects changed to the sounds of musical instruments used in her event.
  - During the Kumamon collabs, the paper dolls would wear Kumamon masks.
  - During Senhime release, sealand shikigami would walk up and down the street in the town.

- The moon in the bamboo forest background (eg; in Chapter 1) will change phases when it is not visible on-screen.
- There are shikigami spectating from the buildings in the town background for Nen;
  - Shiro Douji and Kuro Douji
  - Enenra and Kamikui
  - Youko
- Paper dolls might change appearance while in explore zones;
  - Chef
  - Sushi Chef
  - Pumpkin Head (Halloween)
  - Komatsumaru
  - Sakurako (paper doll mascot for Onmyoji: ARENA)

- When a player's onmyouji is KO'd, their mask will sometimes remain on the battlefield.
- When Youko attacks Kyonshii Imouto, he will shoots hearts at her.
- When Hiromasa attacks, Hakurou has a chance to Assist.
- Kyonshii Ani gives different coffins to his siblings.
- Seimei's passive will cause Kaguya Hime to duck if she is beside or in front of him.
- When Hiromasa shoots an arrow while in a team with Ootengu or SP Ootengu, Ootengu's feathers will trail behind the arrows.
- After SP Ootengu uses his special attack while in a team with Hiromasa, a whirlwind will go behind Hiromasa. This will not happen if Ootengu is in the team.
- When Ibaraki Doji attacks wearing the bamboo skin, the Ibaball will point to the target.
- Some shikigami have special effects when transformed into paper dolls;
  - Kaguya Hime and Susabi's realm indicators
  - Heiyou turns metallic
  - Kyonshii Otouto will vary in size
  - Ungaikyou will constantly look around as if confused
- Koi no Sei's skill 2 gives Kappa a heart-shaped shield.
- When Momiji is placed in a team with Seimei as the onmyouji, hearts will appear above her.
- When Shuten Douji is placed in a team with Momiji, hearts will appear above him.
- When Ibaraki Douji is placed in a team with Shuten Douji, an exclamation mark will appear above Ibaraki Douji.
  - If Ibaraki Douji KO's the last enemy, "..." will appear above Shuten Douji.
- When Momiji is KO'd while in a team with Shuten Douji, an angry emote will appear above him and he'll receive a 10% increase to ATK and a 10% decreases to DEF (both effects are dispellable).
- When Susubi assists an ally while in a team with Miketsu, the visual effect for his attack will change to match her colour scheme.
- When Kochou no Sei and Hitotsume are in the same team, a purple butterfly will appear around him.
- When Orochi substitutes the player's onmyouji in PVP, Practice or Spar he will comment on the opponent's onmyouji.
- When Enmusubi no Kami is placed in a team with certain shikigami, she will comment on them. There are a total of 17 comments she will make.
- When SP Enmusubi no Kami is placed in a team with certain shikigami, she will comment on them.
- When SP Ootakemaru is placed in a team with certain shikigami or with Yaobikuni as the onmyouji, he will comment on them. There is a total of 14 comments he will make.
- When Taishakuten and Asura are placed in a team against one another, they will comment on each other. Taishakuten has a total of 3 comments and Asura has a total of 2 comments.
- When Amaterasu's Heavenly Radiance skill effect links to Susanoo or SP Susabi, the visual effect for it will change to match their colour schemes.

- Ootengu has a chance to take off his mask in his awakened skin.
- When Ootengu is left idle while equipped with his s-jade skin, he'll perform a song with his flute.
  - When left idle in the courtyard, Hiromasa will join him in a duet version of the song.
- Rotating Ungaikyou will cause their icon in Shikigami Records to change from Yang to Ying and vice versa.
- When viewing Ungaikyou with the AR camera via AR Summoning, Yin will not be visible from Yang's side of the mirror.
- Tapping on Kaguya while she has the Scarlet Grace skin equipped has a chance to make a short illustrated animation to play.
- SP Miketsu's background will change from a sun to a moon depending on the time of day.
- Various shikigami will have heart emotes when they appear with characters they are paired with in dispatch;
  - Shuten Douji and Momiji
  - Ibaraki Douji and Shuten Douji
  - Shiro Mujou and Kuro Mujou
  - Momo and Sakura
  - Enenra and Kamikui
  - Yama Usagi and Mouba
  - Doujo and Oguna
  - Hangan and Enma
  - Tesso and Kyuumei Neko

- Saying certain keywords in chat during events or holidays will make emotes appear;
  - "Thanks" during Thanksgiving
  - "Halloween" during Halloween
  - "Kinnara" during her release

  - Aoandon will tell a story
  - Seimei will try to avoid giving daruma
  - Susabi will attempt to destroy the town
  - The people lost in the mist will be found again
