All assembly bosses have a lobby space where players are visually shown to appear. It is accessed before and after the battle, as well as in the middle should a player quit (there is 1 min cooldown).

Players with badges for the associated boss will be denoted in a bottom counter. While up top is typically a way to view the boss's bio.

Players will also have shortcuts to the onmyouji interface and shikigami interface.

Small monsters will also appear at this time, and they can be tapped on to redeem additional rewards.

There are 3 types of rewards: gold, secret treasure, and victory rewards. Gold and victory rewards are given through mail, while secret treasure is instantaneous at the end of battle.

Gold is given dealing damage to the boss, the higher the damage to more the gold, with a cap of 60k gold. The secret treasure gives random rewards. The victory rewards are given if at least 30k damage is contributed, and distributed according to the rating. If not enough damage is contributed, 500 gold is given for reward.

Tsuchigumo (土蜘蛛) is one of the large-scale Rare Bosses found in Demon Encounter.


Tsuchigumo is a large Rare Assembly Boss. It appears for 60 minutes and if it is not defeated within that time, it will flee. Each player can challenge up to 1 Rare or Ultra Boss per day. However, challenging a boss that you discovered won't reduce an attempt. Defeated players can continue to challenge the same Assembly Boss, but must wait 60 seconds before challenging them again. Players can chat with other players fighting the same Assembly Boss in the Team chat channel.

By challenging Tsuchigumo, players have a chance to unlock Marks based on their performance. Players who have obtained the Veteran Spider Buster and Hypersonic Raider Marks can search for and challenge Ultra Tsuchigumo (极・土蜘蛛/極・土蜘蛛).

Once the assembly has begun, players are transported to a assembly grounds where they can read the boss' biography, and access shortcuts to Line-up Pre-set, Onmyoudou and Shikigami Records. Battle begins at the end of the countdown.

The Rare version of the boss has 2 stages: Body Parts and Main Body. The Ultra version has an additional Cocoon stage and offers a weekly rotation of Mutation Skills to players and the boss.

Tsuchigumo must be weakened by damaging its body parts. Doing so also inflicts different debuffs on Tsuchigumo.

It is recommended to target the body parts in this order: Leg > Claw > Carapace.

Tsuchigumo has been weakened, it summons Cocoons to heal its damaged body parts. As long as the Cocoons are present, Tsuchigumo takes greatly reduced damage.

Tsuchigumo is weakened will summons Spiderlings for help. A Treasure Paper Doll will appear among the Spiderlings.
