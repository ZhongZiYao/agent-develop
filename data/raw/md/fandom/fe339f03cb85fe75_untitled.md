Pets
Pet House
Pet Backyard
Courtyard pet is a mechanism that becomes available to players who are level 20 or over. Pets will provide extra rewards for the first daily clear of a soul zone. Note that the pet's interface has no close button - another spot of the courtyard must be tapped to exit it.
Adoption
[
]
A stray animals will appear in the courtyard to be adopted. Each pet can be named upon being adopted.
Interface
[
]
Pets can be fed and played with, their growth can be monitored, and there are options to go to the Pet Backyard or purchase pet accessories.
Hungry
[
]
When pets become hungry, they can be fed some delicious sushi (AP). 3 AP can be used to restore a pet's hunger by 16.
Sad
[
]
When pets become loney, they can be cheered up by playing with a wool ball toy or by resting a soft cushion. Cheering them up with either item restores a pet's happiness by 40.
Growth
[
]
The pets have three ranks for growth in closeness. A visit to a friend's pet house to play with a pet of the same breed is required to progress onto the next rank.
Pet Help
[
]
Pets will support their owners when they are not sad and hungry. They will give a co-op reward for the first clear of any
Orochi
stage per day and their growth in closeness will also increase. They will also give s daily bonus reward for being happy and well fed.

Sumptuous Feast
[
]
Prerequisites: The player must have 4 or more pets.
Multiple pets can be fed at once. Those whose fullness value is above 70 will not continue to eat. The AP consumed in the feast is equivalent to that which would be consumed by feeding the animals separately. This function consumes 1000 gold per use.
Happiness Without Bounds
[
]
Prerequisites: The player must have 4 or more pets.
Multiple pets can play together until their happiness values are 100. Happiness values that are above 100 will not further increase. This function consumes 1000 gold per use.
Every courtyard now has a corresponding backyard. More homeless animals can be added to the backyard by using it. It is accessed by tapping on the "Pet Backyard" button in while in the Pet House or while a pet is selected. Each courtyard skin has a different backyard with different spots for shikigami to stay.
Food
[
]
After shikigami are selected, food can also be presented according to shikigami type. Fresh fish will attract cats, and bones will attract dogs. The higher the grade of the food, the more animals that can be attracted.
Food can be obtained through
Wanted Quests
. High-grade food can be obtained from co-op wanted quests or events.
Stray Animals
[
]
Sometime after setting up shikigami and food, animals will be attracted.
Closeness
[
]
Attracted animals can be petted to increase affection, which is a counter. After achieving max affection, an abandoned animal will appear in courtyard begging to be adopted.
When an animal with maxed affection comes to the backyard, they will leave behind Pawprint Coins.
Shikigami
[
]
Two shikigami from records can be freely set in the backyard, and different shikigami will attract different types of animals: feline shikigami will attract homeless cats, while canine shikigami will attract homeless dogs.
