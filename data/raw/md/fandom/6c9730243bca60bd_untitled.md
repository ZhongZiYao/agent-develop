The Awakening Dungeon, also known as the Evolution Zone or Evo Zone, is the place to get materials needed to evolve shikigami by challenging the kirin on each level. The dungeon is separated into 4 kinds of kirin that give different materials.

Stages[]

List of features unlocked for players at varying levels.

Fire Kirin howls and deals 100% attack damage to one target, causing them to enter flammable state.

Exhalation (吐息)

Type: SpecialOnibi:3Cooldown: 0

Fire Kirin spits out fire to attack all targets, dealing +100% damage to units in inflamed state.

Wind Kirin give Wind Talismans.

Typhoon (飓风)

Type: SpecialOnibi:3Cooldown: 0

Wind Kirin summons the power of typhoons to attack all targets with 100% attack damage.

Water Kirin give Azure Carps.

Skills[]

Water and Wind (水风)

Type: NormalOnibi:0Cooldown: 0

Watersprout (泉涌)

Type: SpecialOnibi:3Cooldown: 0

Thunder Kirin give Lightning Drums.

Skills[]

Sudden Flash (惊雷)

Type: NormalOnibi:0Cooldown: 0

Thunder Kirin calls on the power of lightning and deals 100% attack damage to one target.

Thunderstorm (雷暴)

Type: SpecialOnibi:0Cooldown: 0

Thunder Kirin calls on the power of lightning and attacks all enemies, with 10% chance to daze.

Rewards[]

The higher the stage, the higher the chances of mid and high-grade materials. There is also a chance of Realm Raid tickets, Demon Parade tickets and even Red Daruma to drop as rewards.
