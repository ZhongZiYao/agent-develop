Discipline (戒•定•慧)

Exclusive only. Elephant Drum is awakened and sustained for 3 turns. When Elephant Drum is present, gain 1 onibi, 2 stacks of Fortune Might and increase all allies move bar by 20%.

- Fortune Might (福威)
- **Buff** ,**Mark** : Up to 5 stacks. Each stack increases Effect RES by 30%.
- Heart Drum (心鼓)
- **Buff, Mark**
- Max 6 stacks, upon reaching the limit, remove all stacks and cast Discipline with no cost. This triggers up to 3 times before Bishamonten's next turn.
- Elephant Drum (象鼓)
- While on the field, 50% of the damage Bishamonten takes is evenly shared by other allies as transferred damage. This damage is non-lethal, does not trigger souls or passive effects, and transferred damage cannot trigger again. When triggered, gain 1 stack of Heart Drum.
- Sustain (维持)
- A status or mark will last depending on the number of turns of the originator, and not target.
- Lose (失去)
- Not like receiving damage, lost HP cannot Crit, is not absorbed by shields and does not trigger damage related effects. When a shikigami loses most of their HP and/or continues to lose more HP, their remaining cannot drop below 1 HP.

| Level | Effect | 
|---|---|
| **2** | When Elephant Drum sounds, the 2 enemies with the lowest HP ratio lose 10% of their max HP, up to 100% of Bishamonten's starting ATK. | 
| **3** | When an enemy loses HP, Bishamonten gains 1 stack of Heart Drum (max 6 stacks per action) | 
| **4** | While Elephant Drum is on the field, this unit shares 30% of the single-target damage allies take. | 
| **5** | Upper Hand: Cast Discipline | 


Heavenshaker Staff (撼天伏魔棍)

Consume all Fortune Might to attack the enemy target, dealing 300% ATK damage, with the damage multiplier increasing by 30% for each Fortune Might consumed. After use, before the start of the next turn, Elephant Drum is triggered and move bar is increased by 20%.

- Fortune Might (福威)
- **Buff** ,**Mark** : Up to 5 stacks. Each stack increases Effect RES by 30%.
- Elephant Drum (象鼓)
- While on the field, 50% of the damage Bishamonten takes is evenly shared by other allies as transferred damage. This damage is non-lethal, does not trigger souls or passive effects, and transferred damage cannot trigger again. When triggered, gain 1 stack of Heart Drum.

| Level | Effect | 
|---|---|
| **2** | Damage increased to 330%, with each stack's Fortune Might coefficient boost increased to 35%. | 
| **3** | While Elephant Drum is on the field, onibi cost is reduced by 1. | 
| **4** | Damage increased to 360%, with each stack's Fortune Might coefficient boost increased to 40%. | 
| **5** | If the damage dealt during the attack is absorbed, shared or nullified, immediately gain 3 stacks of Fortune Might and attack the same target 1 more time. This effect does not trigger consecutively. |
